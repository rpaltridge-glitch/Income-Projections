📈 Performance Analytics

Seasonal Performance Patterns

☀️ Peak Season (Jun–Jul, Oct, Dec)
June and July bring consistent family travel demand as groups plan extended summer vacations, while October's fall foliage surge is one of the most reliable occupancy drivers in the Gatlinburg / Pigeon Forge market — making it a critical pricing window. December adds a holiday travel spike that, combined with the indoor pool's all-weather appeal, supports strong rate and occupancy performance even as temperatures drop.
🌸 Shoulder Season (Mar, Aug, Nov)
March, August, and November represent meaningful revenue opportunities where strategic pricing and amenity emphasis can maintain occupancy above the market average — the indoor pool is a particularly effective conversion tool for guests who wouldn't book an outdoor-pool property during these months. The softest stretch — January, February, April, May, and September — calls for targeted short-stay promotions, weekday discounts, and highlighted in-cabin entertainment options to keep occupancy competitive rather than leaving nights unbooked at any rate.

📊 Data Sources

  • • Comp financials: live trailing-twelve-month revenue, ADR and occupancy for each listing shown
  • • Occupancy: derived as revenue ÷ (ADR × 365) so every figure displayed reconciles against the other two

⚠️ Key Assumptions
  • • Year One reflects a genuine launch ramp: a new listing carries no review history and no booking velocity on day one
  • • Stabilized assumes 75% occupancy — a level 22 mature 3-bedroom cabins inside this radius already hold today
  • • Professional management with dynamic pricing and listing optimization
  • • 2081 Kerr Road sits 11.7 miles north, outside this radius — it is an owner-reported benchmark, not one of the comparables above
  • • Ranges reflect the observed spread across comparables, not a guaranteed outcome
💡 Performance Drivers
