How we built these numbers

  • Every comparable is a 3, 4, 5 or 6 bedroom entire home within three miles of Laurel Estates, measured over the last 12 months of AirROI booking data.
  • Revenue is nights booked × average nightly rate. Homes with fewer than 20 reviews are left out of the medians, because new listings are still ramping up.
  • Each projection's nightly rate and occupancy are set slightly above the median of the established homes for that size. Where too few homes trade to price a size directly, it's derived with the indoor-pool lift we measured locally: about an 18% higher nightly rate and 5 more points of occupancy.
  • Laurel Estates' own homes are shown for context and are never used as comps.
  • These are estimates, not a guarantee. Results depend on finishes, views, reviews and owner use.
